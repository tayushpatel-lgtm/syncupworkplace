# Recurring tasks

When you assign a task, **Repeats** follows the Google Calendar-style list. Completing a repeating task marks that occurrence done and opens the next one (same title, person, priority, and repeat settings, with the next due date).

One-off tasks land on My Day as soon as they are open. Repeating tasks only show on the plan on or after the day they are due.

The first due date (or today, if you have not set one) is what the weekly and annual labels use.

## Does not repeat

A one-off. It stays open until someone completes or deletes it. Nothing new is created when it is done.

## Daily

Comes back every calendar day, including Sunday.

## Weekly on [weekday]

Repeats each week on the weekday of the first due date. Completing Monday’s task opens next Monday.

## Annually on [date]

Repeats on the same month and day next year.

## Every weekday (Monday to Saturday)

Comes back on the next working day, Monday through Saturday. Sunday is skipped: completing Friday opens Saturday; completing Saturday opens Monday.

## Custom…

Opens a centred **Custom recurrence** dialog (not extra fields under the select).

- **Repeat every** — a number plus day, week, month, or year.
- **Repeat on** — when the unit is week, the days of the week (Sunday through Saturday). Tick any mix; the company’s weekday preset is Monday to Saturday.
- **Ends**
  - **Never** — the series does not stop on its own.
  - **On** — last day an occurrence may be due.
  - **After** — stop after this many occurrences, counting the first.

Cancel leaves the previous frequency. Done applies the custom rule.
